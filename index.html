The Name

Beatzkut was born in Brooklyn from the two things I've always done: make beatz and kut hair. My people named me for both.

About

Started in Brooklyn in 1997. What began on the block became a career that's taken Danny from neighborhood chairs to international sets. He's worked across music, film, television, and high fashion trusted by talent, sought by production, credited by press.
